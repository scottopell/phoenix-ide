# Resolve outstanding turns automatically after restart

Implement the bounded OWED-BATON recovery contract admitted after #817: a durable outstanding obligation must drive a turn to resolution without browser-open or another user message. Recover queued inputs, tool results, continuations, watches, and startup handoffs with one baton across continuation; honor stop/cancel/newer input/approval; reconcile unknown/running external effects without blind replay; back off interrupted waits; and terminalize repeated crashes explicitly. Truly idle conversations remain idle and prose promises create no durable work.

Scope is runtime/executor/persistence/API mechanics plus normative bedrock/durable-workflow contracts and an ADR where policy changes. Ordinary and Global surfaces are included. Excludes profile/charter/prompt/UI-specific integration owned by #788, profile migrations 113-119 and ADR 074 allocations, unrelated #797 overload work, default concurrency caps, scheduler/platform rewrites, production crash/deploy, and browser-kick behavior. Include deterministic restart regressions and a disposable nondestructive Global deployment-followthrough harness. Global owns final merge/deploy and postdeploy verification.
