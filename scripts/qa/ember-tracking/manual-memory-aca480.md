## Fixed journey and result

1. Save requested work, “Check Cobalt export totals,” and the question bookmark, “Why the Orchard boundary?” Keep their purposes and stopping points distinct.
2. Answer neutral arithmetic without inventory chatter or extra tools.
3. Continue with a deliberately incomplete handoff, then recover both saved items and their stopping points.
4. Answer another neutral arithmetic question quietly.
5. Admit an unrelated greeting with a host-selected overdue request. Inspect that request, preserve its limits, and ask the specific owed period question without the user naming it.

All five stages passed. On the due turn Ember read the current record, applied a revision-checked update from revision 1 to 2, and asked:

The record retained evaluation-only scope and the instruction to await the answer. The bookmark did not become commissioned work. There were no external-effect attempts or provider failures.
