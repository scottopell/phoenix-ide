## Why it exists

The problem it solves, in user terms. What would be painful or impossible
without it.

## How it works

The mental model. Enough to reason about behavior — not the implementation.
Use a small table or list when there are distinct variants or states.

## What you'll see

How the concept surfaces in the UI: badges, panels, transcript items, colors.
Tie abstract ideas to concrete on-screen affordances.
