# Native macOS launchd Deployment Requirements

## Scope

Safe replacement of Phoenix's native macOS production LaunchAgent from exact checked local `HEAD`, an immutable published GitHub release, or an explicitly selected protected upgrade: prepared-artifact paired under REQ-LDD-017 or current-schema under REQ-LDD-019. Linux deployment modes are outside this specification.

## Requirements

### REQ-LDD-001 — Independent activation ownership

When Phoenix initiates a production deployment, the system shall transfer activation to a launchd-owned one-shot helper before stopping the Phoenix LaunchAgent.

### REQ-LDD-002 — Complete preparation before disruption

Before stopping the running service, the system shall validate and stage the candidate binary, complete plist, rollback inputs, embedded identity, signature, and immutable activation manifest on the destination filesystem.

### REQ-LDD-003 — Single activation writer

While an activation or unresolved transaction owns the host deployment claim, the system shall reject another deployment without changing the production service or artifacts.

### REQ-LDD-004 — Immutable, secret-safe handoff

For ordinary sources, the activation helper shall be sourced from the selected immutable commit. For prepared-artifact paired/current-schema sources, it shall be sourced from the separately recorded verified clean controller commit, which may differ from the candidate commit. For the protected published-release current-schema transition under REQ-LDD-019, it SHALL instead be sourced from the explicitly pinned full controller commit, not the candidate release commit; the retained helper path, bytes and SHA-256 SHALL be immutable transaction evidence. The helper shall consume only stable host-resident files and a manifest containing source identity, candidate and previous runtime identities and endpoints, artifact hashes, target paths, and rollback paths; the manifest and durable diagnostics shall not contain plist environment values.

### REQ-LDD-005 — Atomic artifact replacement

When installing a candidate or restoring a rollback artifact, the system shall fsync staged data and replace the target with an atomic rename without first unlinking the live path.

### REQ-LDD-006 — Observed launchd transitions

When changing the target job state, the system shall check launchctl exit status and poll structured job state and PID conditions to a bounded deadline.

### REQ-LDD-007 — Exact runtime verification

A deployment shall succeed only when the target job is running with a new PID and the credential-free `/api/version` endpoint reports both the expected package version and complete 40-character lowercase embedded git SHA. The candidate SHA shall equal the selected full source commit exactly; a matching prefix shall not establish candidate identity.

### REQ-LDD-008 — Verified rollback

Protected current-schema transactions, including published-release source kind, SHALL obey REQ-LDD-021–022's pre-invocation-only predecessor recovery boundary. Qualified ordinary migration activation SHALL obey REQ-LDD-023's candidate-start boundary. The artifact-rollback rules below SHALL NOT override those restrictions.

For prepared-artifact paired transactions, predecessor startup/rollback is conditional on the verified recovery authorization under REQ-LDD-017. If database, ownership, snapshot or captured runtime/configuration proof fails, the system SHALL instead attempt teardown, retain a stopped service and unresolved claim, and report recovery failure; it SHALL NOT bootstrap an unproven predecessor. The unconditional artifact-rollback attempt below applies to ordinary sources.

If activation fails after disruption, the system shall atomically restore and bootstrap the previous binary and plist, verify the captured previous runtime identity at the previous service endpoint, and durably distinguish successful runtime-artifact rollback from rollback failure.

Only in this rollback role, the captured identity of an already-installed previous runtime may contain either a legacy 12-character lowercase git SHA or a full 40-character lowercase git SHA. This allowance shall not admit shortened identity for a candidate, release asset, helper, or general downgrade path and shall not establish cross-version compatibility.

Ordinary runtime-only rollback shall not restore a database or guarantee predecessor compatibility with candidate-mutated data. The explicit paired ProductConversation upgrade shall restore the verified matching database and runtime before predecessor startup under REQ-LDD-017; other rollback behavior remains governed by `specs/compatibility/requirements.md`.

### REQ-LDD-009 — Truthful durable result

After exact verification, the system shall write `deployed.sha` from the selected candidate's embedded source commit and persist a redacted terminal transaction status inspectable after the initiating connection ends.

### REQ-LDD-010 — Recoverable interruption

When status or deployment encounters a stale nonterminal transaction, the system shall expose the transaction and actionable recovery guidance without silently treating it as success. Status SHALL always identify an active claim owner even when the shared last-status record is absent, unreadable, or belongs to another transaction. Active-owner terminal proof SHALL require an owned regular non-symlink status object with explicit literal `finalization_pending` and `rollback_failure` fields, an exact source kind coherent with retained mode evidence, and a recognized terminal state. Any unreadable, empty, non-regular, or symlinked active deploy/restart marker SHALL remain an unresolved ownership fence and SHALL be shown by status.

### REQ-LDD-011 — Explicit candidate sources

The local command shall deploy exact local `HEAD` after checks and compilation and require the candidate to embed that complete 40-character lowercase commit SHA. The release command shall resolve one immutable published tag and its exact commit, select the host-architecture macOS asset, verify its `SHA256SUMS` entry, and require its complete 40-character lowercase embedded git SHA to equal that commit exactly; it shall not run repository checks, dependency installation, worktree mutation, or compilation.

The explicitly selected prepared-artifact source SHALL be admitted only under REQ-LDD-017, without local rebuild or ad-hoc resigning; it SHALL NOT expand ordinary local/release rollback guarantees. Retained mode evidence SHALL use an exact source kind: paired mode requires `prepared_artifact`; current-schema mode requires `prepared_artifact` for the prepared admissions or `published_release` only for the exact-114 transition under REQ-LDD-019; ordinary-forward mode requires `local_head` or `published_release`. Source kind and mode SHALL remain coherent in manifests, status, cleanup, finalization and recovery; missing, unknown, or contradictory evidence SHALL fail closed.

WHEN `latest` is requested,
THE release command SHALL require the resolved tag and GitHub release metadata to identify a stable supported release.

WHEN an exact release-candidate tag is requested,
THE release command SHALL require the tag, GitHub prerelease metadata, full embedded version, and exact commit identity to agree.

### REQ-LDD-012 — Unambiguous command surface

The deployment command shall accept `prod deploy` for local `HEAD`, `prod deploy --release TAG|latest` for ordinary published releases, the complete three-option prepared-artifact paired command under REQ-LDD-017, and explicit protected current-schema selection under REQ-LDD-019, and shall reject positional versions with migration guidance rather than building a local source tag.

### REQ-LDD-013 — Disposable integration safety

A launchd integration harness shall use disposable labels, paths, database, and port and shall structurally refuse the production label and production resources.

### REQ-LDD-014 — Installed-state-preserving restart

When the operator requests a production restart, the system shall restart the loaded socket-activated LaunchAgent with a new PID while preserving the installed binary, plist, environment, listener, and deployed source identity byte-for-byte; it shall not build, run repository checks, read `.phoenix-ide.env`, replace installation artifacts, or unload the target job.

### REQ-LDD-015 — Independent restart ownership and fencing

Before signaling Phoenix, the system shall validate that the running process reports adoption of the launchd-owned socket and transfer restart ownership to a distinct one-shot LaunchAgent whose immutable, secret-free handoff records the expected runtime identity, previous PID, installed artifact hashes, endpoint, and target job identity. Restart and deployment shall share one mutually exclusive host-operation fence.

### REQ-LDD-016 — Exact, truthful restart result

A restart shall require the already-installed runtime to report a complete 40-character lowercase embedded git SHA and shall commit only after launchd reports a new target PID and `/api/version` reports the same exact runtime identity, with the installed artifact hashes unchanged. The rollback-only legacy identity allowance in REQ-LDD-008 shall not authorize restart of a shortened-identity installation. The system shall durably distinguish preparation failure, concurrent rejection, verified success, and failure after signaling; it shall not claim rollback when no installation artifact changed.

### REQ-LDD-017 — Supported prepared-artifact paired deployment

After helper handoff and before quiescing production, the paired helper shall physically reserve private database-plus-WAL snapshot and atomic-restore capacity. Initial reservation failure shall be reported before disruption. After quiesce required size shall be checked against the held reservation: a growth shortfall shall resume only a verified unchanged predecessor under the offline legacy/exclusivity and captured binary/configuration proofs below; otherwise the service shall remain stopped with its recovery claim retained.

Failed paired claims shall not advise marker removal based solely on helper absence. Unresolved paired recovery shall remove the target plist from the auto-loaded LaunchAgents directory into its private transaction quarantine and fsync both directories, preventing later login from automatically starting an unverified runtime. Candidate and predecessor verification shall bootstrap private transaction plists rather than publishing an unverified auto-loaded plist. The candidate plist shall be published only after exact identity verification and durable commit; the predecessor plist shall be published only after matched database authorization and predecessor verification. Publication or reservation-cleanup failure after durable commit shall remain a committed diagnostic and shall not trigger rollback or imply reboot persistence; a pending publication/cleanup diagnostic shall be persisted with the commit and its owned claim retained until both attempts have durably completed; quarantine failure shall be explicit. Reservation creation shall fsync its parent directory. Explicit retained-helper recovery shall verify offline ownership, snapshot/context, matching restoration and predecessor identity before releasing the claim; failed verification shall attempt teardown, preserve the claim, and report unconfirmed teardown.

WHEN an operator supplies `prod deploy --prepared-artifact DIR --expected-full-commit SHA --paired-database-upgrade`
THE SYSTEM SHALL require macOS launchd, require all three options together, reject `--release`, first install, and any database path change, and preserve the installed plist's environment and PATH without changing credential or model defaults.

THE SYSTEM SHALL accept only a protected `prepare-main` receipt for the host architecture whose exact full commit, version, Developer ID signature, hardened runtime, accepted notarization, stapled ticket, Gatekeeper result, embedded-helper equivalence, and standalone SHA-256 bytes all verify. The candidate source kind SHALL be `prepared_artifact`, distinct from the clean controller HEAD source commit. The system SHALL never ad-hoc resign the prepared binary.

THE handoff manifest SHALL structurally record the paired ProductConversation database-upgrade mode, exact controller source commit, helper bytes, captured predecessor binary/plist identities, database path, fixed private backup/proof destinations, and transaction identity. The snapshot proof and evolving rollback/status observations SHALL be separate durable records bound to that immutable manifest; they SHALL NOT be fabricated or added by mutating the handoff. The helper SHALL reject missing or inconsistent fields and SHALL require the controller helper bytes to match the recorded clean controller source and protocol.

After backend-managed quiesce confirms the predecessor stopped, THE helper SHALL prove no other process has the database, WAL, or SHM open using bounded macOS `lsof`, take a SQLite backup-API snapshot into a private mode-700 transaction directory with a mode-600 database, validate integrity, and durably verify the snapshot before candidate startup. It SHALL never raw-copy a live database.

IF candidate activation or health verification fails, THE helper SHALL stop the candidate first, re-prove exclusive offline ownership, restore and integrity-check the matching snapshot while removing stale WAL/SHM only under that proof, atomically restore the predecessor binary/plist, and start the predecessor only after database restoration. If any proof fails it SHALL leave the service stopped, persist actionable recovery status, and retain the active claim. Existing runtime-only rollback remains unchanged. Before a snapshot proof exists, a failure MAY resume the predecessor only after confirmed teardown, exclusive offline ownership, legacy ledger/table eligibility, and captured binary/configuration checksum equality prove that candidate mutation has not occurred. This path SHALL NOT claim database restoration. An interrupted pre-handoff preparation, whether or not its immutable manifest has already been persisted, SHALL release only its matching claim after durable terminal status, a dead recorded preparation PID, and confirmed target helper absence; live, reused, missing, or unproven process identity SHALL retain ownership. Successful commit SHALL remove only the temporary database-adjacent restore reservation and fsync its parent, retaining the audit snapshot/proof.

Before a recovered predecessor can serve requests, THE paired helper SHALL persist a post-restore startup checkpoint and typed snapshot-restored versus unchanged-predecessor outcome. Any retry after that checkpoint SHALL verify/finalize the running predecessor without restoring the snapshot again; absent or unverified predecessor state SHALL fail closed rather than discard subsequently accepted writes.

A failure limited to plist publication or deployed-identity durability after predecessor identity verification SHALL retain the verified running predecessor and unresolved claim for finalization-only retry. This SHALL NOT authorize leaving any unverified candidate/predecessor running; unknown identity/database/start proof SHALL still attempt teardown. Prepared/helper-handoff interruption with dead recorded preparer and confirmed helper absence SHALL terminalize without runtime disruption; manifest presence SHALL NOT authorize rollback.

### REQ-LDD-018 — Committed paired finalization without runtime or database mutation

WHEN the operator invokes `prod finalize-paired TXN` for interrupted committed paired finalization,
THE SYSTEM SHALL use only the retained byte-bound helper and immutable transaction under mutual exclusion, require its matching ownership claim and confirmed activation-helper absence, and verify the running exact committed candidate binary, identity and private loaded configuration before any mutation.

THE SYSTEM SHALL retry only atomic publication of that captured candidate plist and removal/fsync of the transaction-owned temporary restore reservation, retain authoritative audit backup/proof, and persist pending status/claim through interruption or error. It SHALL clear pending and release only the owned claim after durable completion. An already completed rerun SHALL verify that same candidate/configuration and produce an idempotent result without applying ambient configuration.

THE SYSTEM SHALL NOT stop, bootstrap, restart, touch the database, restore an older runtime, or generalize this operation into downgrade or cross-version recovery. Unknown, stale, mismatched, or absent evidence SHALL refuse without releasing ownership.

### REQ-LDD-019: Explicit protected current-schema mode

**Condition:** When an authorized operator selects a prepared artifact to replace a production database whose complete ordered ledger is either the qualified migration-123 baseline or exactly equal to the qualified migration-128 candidate registry,
**System:** the deployment controller SHALL require an explicit current-schema mode distinct from paired legacy upgrade, preserve every prepared-artifact receipt/signature/identity check, bind the retained controller helper and candidate migration registry by exact path and SHA-256, reject release sources except for the exact protected published-release admission below, reject runtime-only sources, and fail closed for a missing, partial, unknown, mismatched, or newer migration ledger.

**Rationale:** Relaxing the legacy eligibility gate would make incompatible database families indistinguishable and would weaken the signed-artifact handoff.

**Condition:** When an authorized operator explicitly selects a published release in protected current-schema mode,
**System:** the controller SHALL admit exactly a source database whose complete ordered ledger equals the candidate registry's complete prefix through migration 114, with no missing, extra, or differently named entries, and exactly the migration-128 target registry qualified by REQ-COMP-012. It SHALL reject prepared/local sources for this admission, release aliases such as `latest`, first install, database path changes, any other source baseline, and any different or newer target registry before disruption.

The public command `prod deploy --release TAG --expected-full-commit CANDIDATE_SHA --controller-source-commit CONTROLLER_SHA --current-schema-upgrade` SHALL require macOS launchd, an exact non-alias tag, distinct complete 40-character lowercase candidate and controller commits, and neither prepared-artifact nor paired mode. It SHALL derive the backend and host asset/checksum from normal release selection without requiring hidden controller flags. Hidden controller-mode invocation SHALL retain its exact release-tag, backend and asset bindings.

The controller SHALL require an exact release tag, expected full release commit, full embedded version and SHA, host-target asset and published checksum to agree; source kind SHALL remain `published_release`. The candidate binary and complete migration registry SHALL come from that release commit. The activation helper SHALL come from a separately explicit full controller commit, not the candidate release commit or ambient checkout; its retained path, bytes, SHA-256 and controller commit SHALL be bound independently in the immutable handoff and re-verified before migration, activation and recovery. This admission SHALL preserve captured installed configuration and the same canonical database path and SHALL traverse REQ-LDD-020–022 and REQ-LDD-024 without a weaker release-specific lifecycle. Prepared exact-123/exact-target admissions and ordinary exact-123-to-128 qualification SHALL remain unchanged; no source range or generic release compatibility is authorized.

**Condition:** When the prepared current-schema candidate receipt, source commit, expected full commit and embedded candidate SHA all equal `23badb8be4bc8d05a7e1c49c5367ceb14ebccd92`,
**System:** the controller SHALL automatically add the closed typed evidence pair `prepared_source_baseline: 114` (integer) and `candidate_registry_expected_sha256: a4eb047e59b21997ea068f1bd5e97c9c943f74a4c5670cdd8ff8ac10ffd5eee6` inside `current_schema_upgrade`. Both fields SHALL be required together only for this qualification, with no extra qualification fields. It SHALL materialize `crates/phoenix-db/src/migrations.rs` by `git show SOURCE_COMMIT:path` from that exact full receipt-bound candidate commit present in the controller repository, and SHALL require both the expected and actual retained registry SHA-256 to equal that fixed digest and the parsed target to equal the exact qualified registry128. The semantic registry is unchanged from RC4; source-kind/candidate admission is explicitly expanded, not generalized.

The helper SHALL verify source kind `prepared_artifact`, that exact candidate/source/expected SHA, the complete typed field combination, registry bytes/digest/target, and a full controller commit different from the candidate before database admission or runtime stop. The controller SHALL require a clean checkout and retain/hash-bind its helper independently; it SHALL NOT require controller equality with the candidate or source registry bytes from the controller working tree. This qualified variant SHALL admit only exact complete prefix114 or exact target128; prefix113 and prefixes115..127 SHALL reject. Manifests without either qualification field SHALL retain only the generic prepared exact-prefix123/exact-target128 policy. A single field, wrong type/value, unknown field, different candidate or source kind SHALL NOT grant source114 admission. Qualification evidence SHALL be bound into snapshot proof and re-verified during recovery/finalization; removal or alteration SHALL fail closed. The public command remains `./dev.py prod deploy --prepared-artifact DIR --expected-full-commit 23badb8be4bc8d05a7e1c49c5367ceb14ebccd92 --current-schema-upgrade`, with no extra admission flag. REQ-LDD-020–022 and REQ-LDD-024 SHALL apply unchanged; once invoked, candidate-only recovery forbids predecessor startup or snapshot replay even on migration/start failure. No other prepared114 admission is authorized.

### REQ-LDD-020: Current-schema snapshot and capacity proof

**Condition:** When current-schema activation is prepared,
**System:** the activation helper SHALL verify the exact qualified migration ledger, required ProductConversation tables, SQLite integrity, and foreign keys before disruption; physically reserve both backup and restore capacity using the observed live database plus WAL size and a growth margin equal to 10 percent of that observation and clamped to 1–8 GiB, capped by each capacity pool's safe ceiling, computed without crediting existing live database or WAL logical bytes against copy-on-write peak demand, while retaining at least 20 GiB free after prepared install overhead; treat APFS volumes in one container as one capacity pool and use the minimum reported free value among its affected paths for every initial, stopped, and post-migration evidence check; budget 2 MiB for the complete durable-evidence lifecycle, including allocation-unit-rounded old and replacement proof generations during atomic publication plus capacity evidence; reject any database proof generation larger than 1 MiB and reject a projected evidence lifecycle exceeding 2 MiB before disruption; prove the floor plus the exact pending atomic evidence peak before publishing either initial capacity evidence or a replacement generation; refuse disruption if adequate capacity cannot be held; stop the service and prove exclusive database ownership; recheck the required size against held capacity while stopped and exclusive, retaining the 20 GiB floor plus the not-yet-resident portion of the evidence allowance and treating that recheck as authoritative; create a SQLite backup snapshot; after offline migration, reject committed activation if actual database-plus-WAL growth exceeds the held target or any affected capacity pool loses the 20 GiB floor or remaining evidence allowance; and bind snapshot hash, logical contents, source and target ledgers, controller, helper, candidate, predecessor, and transaction identity in a private durable proof before running a migration.

**Rationale:** Free-space estimates and a highest-version check do not prove that a multi-gigabyte live database can be restored or that it belongs to the qualified migration lineage.

### REQ-LDD-021: Offline migration commit boundary and rollback prohibition

**Condition:** When a qualified current-schema snapshot exists,
**System:** the activation helper SHALL install the exact candidate binary, invoke that binary's bounded `--migrate-only` operation against the manifest-bound database while exclusive ownership remains proven, and verify the exact target ledger, SQLite integrity, and foreign keys before admitting candidate runtime traffic. It SHALL invoke that offline operation even when the admitted source ledger already equals the target registry, because candidate-owned reconciliation may write and invocation remains the irreversible forward boundary.

**Condition:** When current-schema activation fails before offline candidate migration was invoked and the stopped, exclusively owned database retains the exact source ledger and logical contents,
**System:** the activation helper MAY restore only the matching verified snapshot and predecessor binary/configuration, then SHALL verify predecessor identity before releasing ownership.

**Condition:** When offline candidate migration is invoked, including when it fails, is interrupted, commits only a proper prefix of the target ledger, or leaves its write outcome ambiguous,
**System:** the activation helper SHALL NOT replay the snapshot or start the predecessor, SHALL retain exclusive deployment ownership and the compatible candidate artifacts, and SHALL report whether candidate start/publication is pending or migration repair is required.

**Rationale:** Migrations commit independently: migration 126 retires wake behavior while preserving history, migration 127 changes coordinator-home ownership, and migration 128 adds durable creation checkpoints under REQ-CCR-012. Invocation ends rollback authority because a failed process cannot prove that no migration or reconciliation write committed. Treating a successful, partially committed, or ambiguous migration as reversible could erase committed state or run an incompatible predecessor.

### REQ-LDD-022: Current-schema finalization and recovery

**Condition:** When the exact target ledger was durably verified but candidate start or persistent plist publication did not finish,
**System:** `prod finalize-current <transaction>` SHALL re-verify the retained helper, candidate, private plist, database commit proof, and claim before startup; MAY start only that exact candidate; SHALL NOT replay the snapshot or invoke offline migration again; and SHALL publish the persistent plist and release temporary restore capacity only after candidate identity succeeds. Normal startup MAY perform candidate-owned idempotent initialization and reconciliation because those writes use the already committed forward-compatible runtime.

**Condition:** When activation failed before offline candidate migration was invoked and the stopped, exclusively owned database still has the exact source ledger and logical contents,
**System:** `prod recover-current <transaction>` SHALL use the retained immutable transaction to restore and verify the predecessor, while refusing recovery whenever offline migration was invoked, the database changed, or either fact is ambiguous.

**Rationale:** Current-schema committed-state finalization needs a compatible candidate runtime, whereas paired legacy finalization remains publication-only and never bootstraps a runtime.

### REQ-LDD-023: Ordinary forward activation failure boundary

Ordinary migration admission and registry/helper evidence SHALL apply only when the candidate database path exists. An existing database SHALL remain guarded even without an installed predecessor. A nonexistent candidate database path, including a switch from an installed predecessor's different database path, SHALL remain an ordinary fresh-database deployment with runtime-artifact rollback using the captured predecessor configuration and its old database; no database restoration or cross-version compatibility guarantee is added.

**Condition:** When ordinary launchd deployment detects pending migrations outside an explicitly qualified ordinary interval,
**System:** the controller SHALL reject deployment before disrupting the runtime. It SHALL NOT silently fall back to unqualified runtime-artifact rollback or treat the protected published-release exact-114 transition as ordinary qualification.

**Condition:** When ordinary in-place launchd deployment detects the exact migration-123 baseline and exact migration-128 candidate registry defined by REQ-COMP-012,
**System:** the controller SHALL retain the complete candidate registry and its SHA-256 digest as immutable transaction evidence bound to the exact candidate and helper without requiring a standalone database backup; SHALL run pre-handoff ordinary admission from the retained path/hash-bound staged helper rather than an ambient checkout; SHALL re-verify that evidence before activation; SHALL reject any different, missing, or newer registry; and the helper SHALL preserve ordinary predecessor rollback only for failures before candidate startup is attempted.

**Condition:** When candidate startup is attempted,
**System:** the helper SHALL treat migration 126 (`retire_wake_feature_preserving_history`), migration 127 (`coordinator_owned_work_scope_home`), migration 128 (`product_creation_provisioning_stage`), and coordinator-home initialization as possibly committed even when startup or health fails; SHALL stop the failed candidate when possible; SHALL retain the candidate binary/configuration, transaction evidence, and ownership fence; SHALL NOT launch the predecessor; and SHALL report a forward-only recovery state.
A markerless forward-only status SHALL block a new deployment claim even when its retained manifest is missing or contradicts that status; the controller SHALL preserve the candidate and transaction evidence for ownership diagnosis rather than overwrite or prune them.
A forward-only failed state SHALL never be treated as terminal or release its claim solely because retained manifest evidence is missing, malformed, or contradictory.

**Condition:** When an operator explicitly authorizes `prod resume-forward <transaction>`,
**System:** the controller SHALL re-verify the retained helper, candidate binary/configuration, immutable complete candidate registry and its SHA-256 digest, stopped target, and ownership claim; SHALL start only that exact retained candidate; and SHALL retain the forward-only fence on another failure.

**Rationale:** Migration 126 commits independently and older wake logic does not understand its retirement metadata. Migration 127 plus coordinator-home initialization can durably introduce an environment kind the predecessor resource-authority reader rejects. Migration 128 adds durable creation checkpoints under REQ-CCR-012. A failed health check cannot establish that the database remained predecessor-compatible.

### REQ-LDD-024: Pre-stop reservation cleanup fence

**Condition:** When any pre-stop abandonment or capacity cleanup fails and durability of cleanup of transaction-owned reservation or evidence files is uncertain,
**System:** the helper SHALL record a distinct pre-stop cleanup-pending state and retain the matching deployment claim; it SHALL NOT report durable `precondition_failed` or release that claim while cleanup remains uncertain. An initial failure whose reservation and evidence cleanup is durably complete MAY terminalize as `precondition_failed` without entering cleanup-pending.

**Condition:** When an operator retries a pre-stop cleanup-pending transaction,
**System:** the cleanup-only retry and any pre-handoff abandonment SHALL require the exact retained transaction, matching claim, and requested paired/current-schema mode—persisted in initial status before manifest publication—before any cleanup, terminal status, or claim release; the cleanup-only retry SHALL remove only that transaction's unsnapshotted reservation and evidence files, including owned atomic-write temporaries and SQLite reservation sidecars on both initial reservation abort and explicit cleanup, fsync their parent directories, durably record `precondition_failed`, and only then release and parent-directory-fsync the matching claim. If reservation abort, cleanup, terminal-status durability, claim unlink, or claim-parent fsync fails, the system SHALL retain or restore a visible exact-owner claim, durably return to cleanup-pending where possible, and SHALL NOT report success. It SHALL NOT stop, start, or inspect the runtime, touch database contents or the production plist or binary, perform snapshot recovery, or invoke `recover-current` or `recover-paired`.
The prepared recovery commands SHALL reject cleanup-pending and cleanup-completed transactions without rewriting their cleanup-only state.
A markerless cleanup-pending status SHALL fence deploy, restart, and stop operations until ownership is diagnosed. Any markerless status SHALL fence those operations unless it is a readable object that positively names a nonempty transaction, an exact supported source kind, a recognized terminal state, `finalization_pending` absent or literally `false`, and `rollback_failure` absent or literally `null`; unreadable, invalid, non-object, unknown, and recognized nonterminal statuses SHALL fail closed rather than permit evidence overwrite. The retained helper SHALL request bootout only for the exact generated deployment-helper label verified from its immutable transaction identity; identity rejection SHALL NOT request any bootout.

### REQ-LDD-025: Sealed exact current-schema forward replacement

**Condition:** When the operator explicitly invokes `prod repair-current TX --prepared-artifact DIR --expected-full-commit REPAIR_SHA`,
**System:** the controller SHALL admit only the original/repair pin set in `specs/compatibility/requirements.md` REQ-COMP-015 and the exact original parent transaction qualified there. It SHALL require the original current-schema prepared qualification, parent-owned active claim, committed/pending parent status, original committed target128 proof, SQLite integrity/foreign keys and exact complete target ledger, confirmed service/old-helper/child-helper absence, exclusive database ownership and sufficient retained restore capacity plus the protected safety floor and bounded evidence allowance. It SHALL preserve the exact captured parent private plist, including environment, database and executable target paths, without reading ambient deployment configuration. The repair receipt, host-target standalone checksum, Developer ID signature, hardened runtime, notarization/ticket/Gatekeeper evidence and full embedded build identity SHALL retain ordinary prepared-artifact checks. Candidate registry bytes SHALL come from `git show REPAIR_SHA:crates/phoenix-db/src/migrations.rs`, match the fixed source digest and original complete target128, and SHALL NOT come from controller working-tree bytes. The controller SHALL be clean, recorded by full commit, and different from the repair candidate; retained helper bytes SHALL equal that controller's helper git object.

The controller SHALL use exactly `<parent>/current-schema-repair/`, with private owned non-symlink evidence. Closed protocol-v1 `CurrentSchemaRepairManifest` and `CurrentSchemaRepairStatus` records SHALL reject unknown/missing keys, mistyped fields, invalid identities, path crosswires and state/proof inconsistencies. The immutable manifest SHALL bind parent manifest/status/proof hashes, exact repair identity, separate controller/helper, candidate, receipt, registry, captured plist and the deterministic child helper label. The status hash SHALL bind a byte-exact immutable private child `parent-status.json` copy of the original committed/pending global status, not an authority to rewrite that status. Candidate/helper/receipt/registry/private plist, the parent-status snapshot and initial prepared status SHALL be durably retained before the manifest is atomically sealed. After full admission and durable staging but before sealing, the controller SHALL accept only the exact installed original binary or its exact non-executable empty stopped fence, atomically install that fence, remove/fsync the published target plist, and reconfirm service absence. This SHALL block historical retained finalizers and login/reboot persistence without modifying private parent evidence. A valid sealed child SHALL be the selection boundary; no parent pointer or child claim SHALL be introduced. Unsealed partial artifacts SHALL have no activation authority; an interrupted stopped fence SHALL retain the original claim and be accepted only by another exact preparation attempt. Known private owned staging MAY be cleaned only under the original operation lock, before any repair checkpoint, after validating the exact owned paths; unknown evidence, a seal or a checkpoint SHALL refuse cleanup.

**Condition:** When the retained helper activates or `prod resume-current-repair TX` resumes that child,
**System:** it SHALL use retained bytes only, acquire the original operation lock, verify the original manifest/proof and child parent-status snapshot are unchanged, cross-check the live global status with that snapshot until child terminal commit, and verify the exact parent claim still owns unresolved work, and refuse stale helpers, configuration drift, insufficient capacity or non-target databases. Before any failure teardown, it SHALL independently require the fixed Phoenix service label and that label's canonical owning-user `~/Library/LaunchAgents` target, so mutable child or parent evidence cannot redirect stop or quarantine to another service or path. It SHALL confirm service absence, or stop and confirm absence for an interrupted child, and prove exclusive database ownership before install/reconciliation/start admission. It SHALL install and revalidate only the exact repair candidate; durably persist `reconciliation_started` before invoking that candidate's bounded `--migrate-only`; then verify exact target128, integrity, foreign keys and capacity, persist a child target proof and `target_verified`, and persist `start_attempted` before starting only the child private plist. Resume after a verified target checkpoint SHALL NOT rerun reconciliation. An interrupted reconciliation MAY rerun only that same sealed repair candidate. No path SHALL replay a database snapshot or install/start the original candidate or predecessor.

After exact repair identity/health and loaded private-configuration verification, the helper SHALL persist child `committed` with finalization pending, durably publish the repair deployed source SHA and child plist, atomically retire/fsync the publication temporary into retained transaction-owned quarantine without pathname deletion, remove/fsync only the owned original restore-reservation path, retain original backup/proof and child proof, persist terminal child `committed`, and only then release/fsync the exact original claim. Claim-unlink/fsync failure SHALL retain or restore visible exact parent ownership and remain resumable without reconciliation replay, including after reservation cleanup. A terminal rerun SHALL verify the same installed binary, running identity, captured loaded plist, persistent publication and deployed SHA without database/runtime mutation, except completing a retained exact-owner claim release.

Any reconciliation/install/start/health/publication/cleanup ambiguity SHALL use a bounded sequence of atomic renames to isolate each target plist observed during settlement into transaction-owned quarantine without reading or deleting it, fsync the containing directory, attempt repair-candidate teardown, then repeat bounded target isolation and fsync after stop, and reconfirm exact process absence after that final persistence pass. That final probe SHALL determine process-absence truth even when an earlier stop or absence probe was ambiguous. After authenticating the sealed child, resume SHALL enforce bounded quarantine-entry count and aggregate bytes during enumeration and each admission pass while reserving enough entry and byte headroom for both publication retirements and both bounded failure-settlement passes; a failure-state retry MAY consume that already-reserved capacity. Publication SHALL use no-replace semantics, refuse to overwrite target bytes, and atomically retire rather than pathname-delete its temporary so a replacement remains preserved and blocks success.
